# Data Processing Agreement — template (BJ-09)

> **Template, not legal advice.** Have a lawyer review it for your company and the countries you
> sell in before you offer it to customers. Square brackets are to be filled in.

**Between** [Customer legal name], the *controller*, and [LedgerFlow operating company], the
*processor*, for the LedgerFlow service.

1. **Subject.** The processor processes personal data only to provide LedgerFlow (bookkeeping,
   invoicing, payments, bank feeds, email of documents) on the controller's documented
   instructions, which are this agreement and the controller's use of the service.
2. **Data and people.** As listed in `docs/privacy/PERSONAL_DATA.md`: users of the controller's
   workspaces; the controller's customers, vendors and employees as entered in the books.
3. **Confidentiality.** People with access are bound to confidentiality; access is limited by
   role and logged in the audit trail.
4. **Security.** Encryption in transit; per-workspace access rules in the database (row level
   security, tested); two-step login available; backups; role checks on every change.
5. **Sub-processors.** Those in `SUBPROCESSORS.md`. The processor gives [30] days' notice of a new
   one; the controller may object and end the service.
6. **Help with requests.** The service lets people download their data and delete their account;
   the processor helps with other requests within [10] working days.
7. **Breaches.** The processor tells the controller without undue delay and within [48] hours of
   becoming aware of a personal data breach, with what is known.
8. **End of service.** The controller can download a full backup at any time. After the workspace
   is deleted its data is purged after 30 days; the audit trail is kept as stated in
   `RETENTION.md` [or: deleted after [N] years].
9. **Audits.** The processor makes available the information needed to show compliance
   [and allows audits once a year with [30] days' notice].
10. **Transfers.** Data is hosted in [the US]; transfers rely on [Standard Contractual Clauses /
    the EU–US Data Privacy Framework].
